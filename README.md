
# 🏨 Hospitality Analytics.

A dynamic, interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze hotel bookings, revenue, occupancy, cancellations, and property performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Short Description / Purpose

The Hospitality Analytics Dashboard analyzes **134,590 hotel bookings**, **₹2.01B revenue generated**, and **₹1.71B revenue realized** across multiple properties, room categories, and booking platforms.

The dashboard helps management understand **occupancy trends, revenue performance, cancellation patterns, and underperforming properties**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

📊 **Power BI** — Dashboard and data visualization  
📂 **Power Query** — Data transformation and MySQL connection  
🧠 **DAX** — KPI and business metric calculations  
🗄️ **MySQL & SQL** — Data storage, cleaning, validation, and analysis  
📁 **CSV** — Source datasets

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **134,590 hotel bookings** covering:

- Property and hotel details
- Booking dates and platforms
- Room categories
- Booking status
- Revenue generated and realized
- Room capacity and successful bookings

## 🎯 Business Problem

Hotel management needed better visibility into hotel performance and the factors affecting revenue and occupancy.

Key business questions were:

- Which properties are performing best?
- Where is occupancy low?
- How much revenue is actually realized?
- Which room categories generate the most revenue?
- What is the impact of cancellations?
- How can weekday occupancy be improved?

## 📈 Key KPIs

- **Total Bookings:** 134.59K
- **Revenue Generated:** ₹2.01B
- **Revenue Realized:** ₹1.71B
- **Occupancy:** 57.87%
- **ADR:** ₹12.70K
- **RevPAR:** ₹7.35K
- **Cancellation Rate:** 24.83%
- **Revenue Realization:** 70.1%

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Weekday Occupancy Gap
Weekend occupancy was **73.58%**, compared with **51.34% on weekdays**, creating a **22.24 percentage-point gap**.

### 2. Property Performance
**Atliq Blu** achieved the highest occupancy at **62.02%**, while **Atliq Seasons** had the lowest at **44.62%**.

### 3. Cancellation Challenge
The overall cancellation rate was **24.83%**, creating an opportunity to improve booking and revenue realization.

### 4. Room Performance
**Elite rooms** generated the highest revenue at approximately **₹560M**, while **Presidential rooms** achieved the highest ADR at approximately **₹23K**.

### 5. Revenue Realization
Hotels generated approximately **₹2.01B**, of which **₹1.71B was realized**, highlighting the impact of cancellations and other booking outcomes.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Increase weekday demand through corporate and business packages
- Investigate low-performing properties
- Analyze high-cancellation booking platforms
- Optimize pricing using ADR and RevPAR
- Promote high-value room categories
